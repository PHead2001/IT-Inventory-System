## Common acceptance gates and remaining prerequisites

- **Data/access:** preserve caller authorization, active membership/RLS, immutable logical locations, append-only atomic history, actor/version checks and idempotency. Private palette/peek/search/export reads and direct mutation attempts need viewer/inactive/nonmember proof; S0's role exclusions do not waive security gates. New integrity/query/rendering contracts receive principal decisions and required independent review before acceptance.
- **Fixtures/environments:** before nonproduction writes, verify exact candidate and isolated PR backend using supported tooling. Use minimal new owned fictional fixtures/accounts; preserve every original S0 fixture, intentionally colliding scan identities, retained form history, pending photos, standing account and original local label queue. Clean only owned fixtures through normal audited actions. No production writes, migration application, promotion, staging force-move, release-gate changes or unapproved services/purchases.
- **Visual/motion:** full motion requires all listed effects, with one shared layer/intensity controls. Effective OS or app reduced motion immediately shows final states everywhere. Micro-feedback ≤150ms; UI transitions ≤250ms opacity/transform; disclosure height exception ≤250ms; stagger ≤30ms/item and ≤400ms total; tickers 400–600ms; spatial 400–900ms interruptible; theme 450–600ms; ambient ≥20s and hidden-tab pause. No delayed errors/focus, settled layout shift, moving hit areas or >3Hz flashing. Fine-hover effects are excluded on touch. Review combined W01/W02/W03/W04/W10/W15 on Overview, Inventory, record and Spaces in both themes with normal effects enabled.
- **Experience proof:** populated/sparse/long/pending/error/read-only states; desktop plus 375/390px and 1180×757 designer; keyboard/AT semantics, dark/light/System and full/effective-reduced modes. Actual recordings establish route/Back morph, panels, batches, scanning, camera/opening, themes and peeks; screenshots/package presence alone cannot establish motion. Independent hands-on experience review is required before final sign-off.
- **Performance/hardware:** compare equivalent production builds with normal effects; distinguish cold navigation/warm revisits, bundles, main-thread/lookup latency, CLS, active frame cost and idle renders. Evidence identifies SHA, browser, renderer/DPR, hardware, method and sample limits. Emulated phones or disabled WebGL cannot certify real phone/high-DPR/GPU/camera/scanner/screen-reader/physical printing. QR/Code128 pixel decoding and browser PDF dimensions/pages are separate from A307 physical proof. S0 lookup observations are reproduction clues, not a latency benchmark.
